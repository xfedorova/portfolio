  • X5 CRM B2E

    2026

    Product design

    2026

    Art director:
    Alexander Borodin

    Designers:
    Anna Shestakevich
    Daria Kondratenko
    Ksenia Afanasyeva
    Sergey Breus
    Igor Kranga

    Brief

    Extend the internal X5 CRM for employee tasks and requests. Bring existing processes into one interface and simplify complex flows.

    Role

    Product designer on the X5 team, staffed via ONY for 5 months. Designed flows for employees and managers, extended the design system, and prepared research prototypes.

    Key flow — employee hiring

    Reworked a multi-step hiring request for Russian citizens and foreign nationals. It had lived as a separate questionnaire.

    Problem. The form was dense with text, and document requirements changed often. Employees struggled to choose a work schedule.

    Research. Usability testing showed that choosing shifts took too long. New hires did not understand schedule codes and could not choose with confidence.

    Solution. Rebuilt the form into clear steps and reworked the first day and schedule selection. Employees now set shift preferences, and managers see them when reviewing the request.

    Other flows

    • Managers review requests, return them for corrections, and comment on specific fields.
    • Sick leave, days off, and shift swaps, approved by a manager or a colleague.
    • Employees send and sign documents.

    Outcome

    The updated hiring flow is live. Employees can set schedule preferences, and managers see them when reviewing a request.
